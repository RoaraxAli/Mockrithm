Billing Cycles

## Billing & Plans
          

          
            Mockrithm supports three tiers. You can choose to bill monthly or select annual billing to save 20%.
          



          
- **Freemium (Tier 1)**: 1 AI Voice session, standard pacing feedback.
- **Premium (Tier 2 - Popular)**: $10/mo. Unlimited voices, 6 templates, fillers tracking.
- **Pro (Tier 3)**: $25/mo. System designer, custom templates, API access.
