## 1. Annual vs. Monthly Cost Comparison

Annual billing charges a single yearly fee, reducing your total cost compared to monthly payments:

| Plan Tier | Monthly Billing Equivalent | Annual Billing (Prepaid) | Total Savings (Yearly) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Premium** | `$10/mo` | `$96/year` (`$8/mo` equivalent) | **Save $24 / year (20%)** |
| **Pro** | `$25/mo` | `$240/year` (`$20/mo` equivalent) | **Save $60 / year (20%)** |

---

## 2. Upgrading to Annual Billing

If you have an active monthly subscription, follow these steps to upgrade to annual billing:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Billing Settings
    Navigate to your **User Dashboard > Account Settings > Billing & Subscriptions**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Switch Billing Interval Toggle
    In the pricing grid, switch the interval toggle from **Monthly** to **Annually**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Confirm Plan Upgrade
    Click **Upgrade to Annual** on your target tier. Stripe will calculate the pro-rated cost for the remainder of your active billing period.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Process Payment
    Confirm the update inside the Stripe billing portal to apply the changes to your subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## 3. Important Billing Policies

- **Pro-rated Billing:** When switching from monthly to annual billing, Stripe automatically applies any unused monthly balance as credit toward your first annual invoice.
- **Auto-Renewal:** Annual subscriptions renew automatically at the end of the 12-month billing period. You can disable auto-renewal at any time through the billing dashboard.
