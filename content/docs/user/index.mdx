Welcome to the **Mockrithm** user documentation! This guide helps you navigate our career intelligence and mock interview suite.

## Getting Started

1. **Dashboard:** Monitor your scoring history and pacing metrics.
2. **Resume Analyzer:** Upload your CV to check compatibility and ATS scoring.
3. **Mock Interviews:** Start voice-driven preparation sessions tailored specifically to you.

---

### Tips for Success
- Use a headset to minimize background noise during recording.
- Speak naturally and aim for 120-150 words per minute.
