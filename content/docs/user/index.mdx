## 2. Best Practices for Interview Success

To get the most out of your voice practice sessions, implement these settings:

<Callout type="info" title="Microphone Quality">
Use a dedicated headset or external microphone rather than a laptop speaker. Minimizing background echo ensures high-accuracy speech transcription.
</Callout>

### Vocal Pacing Target Guide

Vocal pacing plays a major role in communication scores. Monitor your Words-Per-Minute (WPM) using the metric ranges below:

| Pacing Range (WPM) | Status | Assessment | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Below 110 WPM** | Slow | Low articulation speed | Try to speak slightly faster to keep the interviewer engaged. |
| **110 - 150 WPM** | **Optimal** | Professional pacing | Perfect speed! Maintain this pacing in live interviews. |
| **Above 150 WPM** | Fast | Rushed delivery | Slow down. Pause between key sentences to project confidence. |
