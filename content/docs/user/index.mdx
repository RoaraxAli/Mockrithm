Welcome to the **Mockrithm** user documentation! This manual is designed to help job seekers, students, and professionals navigate the platform and maximize their success using our career intelligence tools.

## Key Features

### 1. Interactive User Dashboard
Your central hub for tracking your preparation progress:
- **ATS Score Tracker:** View historical scores of your uploaded resumes and check how your scores improve over time.
- **Interview Statistics:** Monitor the number of mock interviews completed, average feedback scores, and performance trends.
- **Recent Activities:** Quick access to your active resume workspaces and recently completed interview feedback reports.

### 2. AI Resume Analyzer & Workspace
Tailor your resume for specific job descriptions to pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS):
- **Upload Resume:** Upload your CV in standard PDF format.
- **ATS Compatibility Scoring:** Get instant, detailed feedback on formatting, keyword density, section organization, and grammatical style.
- **Resume Workspace:** Edit your resume sections dynamically in our interactive workspace, using AI-driven autocomplete suggestions to improve impact.
- **Job Tailoring:** Input a target job description, and the AI will scan your resume for keyword gaps, recommending exactly which skills and phrasing to add.

### 3. Voice-Driven Mock Interviews
Practice realistic mock interviews powered by advanced conversational AI:
- **Customizable Sessions:** Choose your target role, industry, seniority level, and focus area (behavioral, technical, or general).
- **Interactive Audio:** Speak your answers naturally. The platform records, transcribes, and analyzes your responses in real time.
- **Detailed Calibration:** Calibrate your audio input prior to starting to ensure high-accuracy voice recognition and clear recording.

## Best Practices & Tips

> [!TIP]
> **Audio Setup:** Use a dedicated headset or lapel microphone to minimize background echo and keyboard clicking, ensuring the transcriber captures your answers clearly.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Pacing & Clarity:** Speak at a natural pace (aim for 120-150 words per minute). Clear articulation yields the highest transcription accuracy and allows our pacing evaluator to provide the best advice.

### Pacing Reference Table

| Pacing Range (WPM) | Status | Action Recommended |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Below 110 | Slow | Try to speak slightly faster to keep the interviewer engaged. |
| 110 - 150 | **Optimal** | Perfect speed! Maintain this pacing during real interviews. |
| Above 150 | Fast | Slow down. Pause between sentences to project confidence. |
