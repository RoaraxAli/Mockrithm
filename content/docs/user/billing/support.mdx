## 2. Step-by-Step Bug Reporting

If you encounter an issue, follow these steps to file a report:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Document the Issue
    Note the page URL and take a screenshot of the error if possible.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Note Your System Settings
    Note your browser (e.g. *Chrome 122*), operating system (*macOS Sonoma*), and microphone model.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Export System Logs
    If you experience audio connection issues, copy the error logs from your browser's Developer Tools Console (`F12` or `Option+Cmd+I`).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Submit Your Support Ticket
    Submit your request using the contact form in the support portal or email details to **support@mockrithm.me**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. General Self-Help Resources

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Resetting Active Audio Calibration">
    If the voice engine stops transcribing your answers mid-session:
    1. Close the active interview panel.
    2. Clear your browser cache and cookies.
    3. Re-run the audio calibration test in the settings panel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stripe Payment Processing Delays">
    Stripe subscription updates usually sync within **2-3 seconds**. If your dashboard does not reflect your upgraded plan within a minute, refresh the page to trigger a manual sync check.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
