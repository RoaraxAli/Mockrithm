## 1. Plan Options

To choose the plan that best fits your goals, consider your preparation timeline:

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="When to choose the Freemium Plan?">
    Best for testing out the platform. Allows you to run a single mock interview and test the microphone calibration settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When to choose the Premium Plan? (Recommended)">
    Best for active job seekers. Unlocks unlimited mock interviews, real-time STAR method validation, and vocal filler count tracking.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When to choose the Pro Plan?">
    Best for senior developers and candidates applying to leadership roles. Unlocks API integration features and custom resume templates.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## 2. Upgrading & Downgrading Processes

Plan updates are managed securely through Stripe Checkout:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Billing Settings
    Navigate to your **User Dashboard > Account Settings > Billing & Subscriptions**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose Your Tier
    Click the **Upgrade** or **Downgrade** button under the plan you want to select.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Complete checkout on Stripe
    The platform redirects you to Stripe's secure portal to enter payment details or confirm plan modifications.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Automatic Upgrade
    Stripe syncs the transaction details with Mockrithm, and your account is upgraded automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## 3. Plan Changes Billing Rules

- **Downgrades:** If you downgrade, your premium features remain active until the end of your current billing cycle.
- **Refund Policy:** Purchases are subject to our **Refund Policy** details. Contact support within 7 days of upgrade if you require assistance.
